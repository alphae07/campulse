Questions people ask

Who can join?

Any student with a matric number. Sign up, fill in your card, and you're live straight away.

Does it cost anything?

No. Browsing and listing are both free.

How do I hire someone?

Open their card, copy their WhatsApp number and message them. You agree the price and deadline with them directly. Campulse doesn't take a cut or handle payments.

Can I stop getting messages while I'm in exams?

Switch your availability to "Busy with Exams" in your dashboard. Your card stays up, but people can see you're not taking work.

Can I remove my profile?

Yes. Delete it from your dashboard and your card and login are gone.
